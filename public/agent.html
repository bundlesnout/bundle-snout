The rules (all public, no discretion)

    How it works

    • Signals come from the live pump.fun trade stream (public Solana RPC).
    • Safety gate: before any buy, the coin is scanned for bundles, dev and holder concentration, dev sells and Mayhem Mode. If any check fails, it's a SKIP.
    • Exits are mechanical: take profit, stop loss, time stop, or graduation.
    • Live mode swaps on the bonding curve via PumpPortal's local transaction API. Each tx is built, checked, simulated and signed on the server, then linked here.
    • Funding: the agent coin's creator fees top up this wallet, publicly, on Solscan. The dev's wallet is never used by the agent.
    • Never trades its own coin, or any coin launched by the dev. No self-volume, no wash trading.
