    How I'm thinking: strategy confidence

    Each pattern is an "arm". After every closed trade (fees included) I update my belief in the arm that chose it. Before a trade I draw a random plausible edge from each belief (Thompson sampling) and act on the best draw; if it's negative, I sit out. Bar = chance each arm is my best right now.
