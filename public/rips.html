FAQ

Is this a giveaway?

No. The dev keeps every card. Holders get no cards, prizes, draws, airdrops or share of anything. It is a public pack-rip log paid for by creator fees.

Where do the fees go?

pump.fun pays 0.30% of every trade (bonding curve) to the creator's vault; after graduation PumpSwap pays the creator fee on its own tier table. The dev claims it and spends it on Collector Crypt packs. "Packs paid from fees" should never be more than this coin's fees; packs the dev pays out of pocket are labelled "paid by dev, not fees".

Why should I trust the numbers?

You don't have to. This page reads everything from the chain: this coin's fee comes from the creator fee recorded in every single trade, packs come from the dev wallet's own purchase transactions, cards from their on-chain records. Every row links to Solscan so you can check it yourself. The raw data is at /api/rips/status.

Is there a bundle or a team allocation?

No. One small dev buy from the creator wallet, no other wallets, no presale, no paid promoters, Mayhem mode off.

Does the dev sell the cards?

The plan is to keep them. Collector Crypt allows selling a card back within 72 hours; if that ever happens, the card is marked "sold back" here.

Is this investment advice?

No. Memecoins are extremely risky and can go to zero. Buying the coin gives you nothing except the coin.
