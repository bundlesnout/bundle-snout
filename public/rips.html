How it works

  1. Every trade of the coin on pump.fun pays a 0.30% creator fee to the dev's creator vault (pump.fun rules, not ours).
  2. The dev claims those fees and uses them to open Collector Crypt card packs inside pump.fun.
  3. Each rip is posted on X with its transaction, and this page reads it straight from the chain: pack, card and proof.
  4. The dev keeps the cards. Holders get no cards, prizes, draws or share of anything. It is a public pack-rip log, nothing more.
